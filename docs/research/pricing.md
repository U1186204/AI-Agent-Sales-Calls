# Oasis demo pricing research (2025-2026 US)

Synthetic but realistic numbers for the Oasis demo catalog. All prices are USD. Service prices are **demo list prices**: picked near the middle of published national ranges, then rounded. Goods prices are rounded from typical big-box or supply-house retail listings (late 2025 to 2026). Weights are approximate shipping weights, including packaging.

> Demo data only. Real prices vary by region, season and vendor. Do not present these as quotes from any real company.

## 6. Sales tax: demo rule

**Background:** 45 states plus DC tax tangible goods by default. Alaska, Delaware, Montana, New Hampshire and Oregon have no statewide sales tax. Service taxation is fragmented:

- About 17 states plus DC tax janitorial/cleaning services in some form (e.g. NY, TX, OH, PA, NJ, FL, CT, MD, MN). Some of these apply only to commercial buildings or above revenue thresholds.
- PA and OH tax routine lawn care.
- HI, NM, SD and WV tax most services by default, which would include TV mounting.
- Combined state+local rates average roughly 7.5% nationally (Tax Foundation).

[ISSA], [Avalara], [Pronto], [Ohio DOT], [Trout CPA]

### Proposed demo rule (one rule, applied everywhere)

| Line type | Taxed? | Rate |
|---|---|---|
| Goods (catalog SKUs: electrical, plumbing) | Yes | **7.5% flat demo rate** |
| Materials/hardware sold within a service (e.g. TV mount supplied, outlet parts) | Yes | 7.5% |
| Labor / service fees (base service, labor add-ons, surcharges) | No | 0% |
| Shipping (separately stated) | No | 0% |
| Discounts | Applied before tax, to the line they reduce | n/a |

`tax = round_half_up(0.075 * sum(taxable line amounts after discounts), 2)`. Compute tax on the order subtotal, not per line, so rounding stays predictable.

**Justification:**
1. It matches the majority pattern. Goods are taxable almost everywhere, and residential labor for these three services is untaxed in most states.
2. 7.5% sits near the national average combined rate, so totals look realistic.
3. Separating "materials within a service" is how states that exempt labor still tax parts. It also gives the chatbot a meaningful line-item distinction to explain.
4. One flat rate avoids address-based rate lookup, which a demo should not pretend to do. The UI and receipts should say: *"Demo tax: 7.5% on goods and materials; labor and shipping untaxed. Real tax depends on your location."*

## 7. Ground shipping model

**Market reference (UPS Ground 2026 daily list rates):** Zone 2 costs $11.99 for 1 lb, $15.91 for 10 lb and $42.00 for 50 lb. Zone 8 costs $15.03 for 1 lb, $26.29 for 10 lb and $124.00 for 50 lb. Contractors with negotiated accounts typically pay 20-40% less. [UPS daily rates], [idshipthat UPS], [atoship]

### Warehouse

**Indianapolis, IN 46241**: a central-US logistics hub, so most of the lower 48 states fall within zones 2-6, and the West Coast is zone 8.

### Destination zone (by first digit of destination ZIP)

| ZIP starts with | Region | Demo zone |
|---|---|---|
| 46-47 | Indiana | 2 |
| 4 (other) | OH, MI, KY | 3 |
| 6 | IL, MO, KS, NE | 3 |
| 1, 2 | NY, PA, DE, DC, MD, VA, WV, NC, SC | 4 |
| 3 | GA, FL, AL, TN, MS | 4 (FL 32-34: 5) |
| 0 | New England, NJ, PR excluded | 5 |
| 5 | MN, WI, IA, Dakotas, MT | 5 |
| 7 | TX, OK, AR, LA | 5 |
| 8 | CO, AZ, UT, NM, NV, ID, WY | 7 |
| 9 | CA, OR, WA | 8 |

AK (995-999), HI (967-968), and territories are not served in the demo (zone 8 rates do not apply).

### Rate formula

`billable_lb = max(1, ceil(total_weight_lb))`
`shipping = base[zone] + per_lb[zone] * billable_lb (+ $25 per item over 50 lb)`

| Zone | Base | Per lb | 1 lb | 10 lb | 50 lb |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | $8.50 | $0.45 | $8.95 | $13.00 | $31.00 |
| 3 | $9.00 | $0.55 | $9.55 | $14.50 | $36.50 |
| 4 | $9.75 | $0.70 | $10.45 | $16.75 | $44.75 |
| 5 | $10.50 | $0.90 | $11.40 | $19.50 | $55.50 |
| 6 | $11.25 | $1.10 | $12.35 | $22.25 | $66.25 |
| 7 | $12.00 | $1.30 | $13.30 | $25.00 | $77.00 |
| 8 | $12.75 | $1.55 | $14.30 | $28.25 | $90.25 |

These sit about 25-30% below UPS daily list prices, consistent with a contractor-account discount.

**Rules:**
- **Large-item handling:** +$25 per item over 50 lb (mirrors UPS Additional Handling). Single items over 150 lb ship as freight and need a quote; none in the catalog qualify.
- **Free shipping:** goods subtotal of **$249+** (before tax) ships free in zones 2-8. Items over 50 lb are excluded and still pay their weight-based share plus handling.
- **Services** carry no shipping. Materials supplied with a service travel with the technician.
- Round shipping to cents. Shipping is not taxed under the demo rule (section 6).

**Example:** to Denver (zone 7), one Romex 12/2 (19 lb) plus 10 GFCIs (4 lb) gives 23 lb, so $12.00 + $1.30 x 23 = **$41.90**. The goods subtotal is $419, which is over $249, so shipping is **$0**.
